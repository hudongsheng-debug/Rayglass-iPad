# Rayglass Privacy Policy


Developer / operator: Dongsheng Hu
Privacy contact: hudongsheng356@gmail.com

This policy applies to Rayglass for Mac and Rayglass iPad. We respect your control over your optical project files and personal information.

## 1. Data Processing in the App

Rayglass does not require an account. The current version does not include advertising, third-party behavioral analytics, or tracking SDKs. The app does not proactively transmit your project contents, device identifiers, or usage activity to developer-operated servers.

Optical prescriptions, material parameters, and calculations are processed on your device. The app stores a recovery copy in its local container so you can resume your work. It reads files you choose to open or import and writes exported content to a location you select.

## 2. Files, Clipboard, and Permissions

File access uses the system file selection workflow. When you choose to paste parameters, the app reads the clipboard text you request to paste and uses it to populate optical parameters. The app does not continuously monitor your clipboard in the background.

Current features do not require camera, microphone, location, or contacts access.

## 3. Cloud Storage and Device Backups

If you choose iCloud Drive or another file service as a destination, or enable system backups, that service may store, synchronize, or back up files according to your settings. Rayglass does not operate a cloud project storage service or provide automatic cross-device synchronization. Those services process data under their own policies.

## 4. External Links

Material-source links and other external links may open third-party websites. Those websites and your browser may process IP addresses, access logs, or other information according to their policies and your settings. Rayglass does not proactively upload your optical project when you follow these links.

## 5. When You Contact Support

If you contact support by email, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send. We process them only to respond, investigate issues, and maintain necessary support records. Email providers may process communications. Send only information needed to resolve the issue, and do not send confidential projects without authorization.

We retain support information for the time needed to address your request and provide reasonable follow-up support, unless a longer period is legally required. You may contact the email address above to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information in support communications.

## 6. Sharing and Tracking

We do not sell personal information or use app data for advertising tracking. The app does not automatically share project files with the developer or third parties. Processing by file services you select, external websites, and support communication services is described above.

## 7. Managing and Deleting Local Data

You can manage and delete saved projects and exports using Finder or Files. Recovery copies are stored in the app container; contact support for instructions appropriate to your system if you need to remove them. Deleting a local file may not delete cloud backups or other copies. Check the relevant services as well.

## 8. Apple Platform Services

App Store downloads, payments, system diagnostics, and processing controlled by system settings are governed by Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies. Statements in this policy about the app not proactively uploading data describe Rayglass's own implementation; they do not replace Apple's policies or those of file service providers.

## 9. Policy Updates

If app features or data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date and provide additional information where appropriate.
